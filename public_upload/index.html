STILLMRK / The Distance That Remains

The Distance That Remains

These photographs move through roads, shorelines, winter fields, and built edges where a person can appear small without disappearing. Trees return as witnesses.

Water reflects what cannot be held for long. Snow removes excess and leaves only relation: between one figure and another, between the body and the land, between solitude and the brief forms of solidarity that interrupt it. Made across different countries and years, the work is held together less by geography than by a recurring condition of distance - not as emptiness, but as a way of being present.

A narrative held together by recurrence rather than chronology.

Each series follows a different condition of distance: threshold, horizon, weather, or winter passage. The images are grouped by pressure and relation, not by file date.

Witness Horizon

Across water, open ground, and horizon lines, human figures remain small without disappearing. Trees recur as witnesses, reflections double what cannot be held, and distance becomes the real subject. These images are less about event than about how presence lands inside open space.

Distance, reflection, witness trees, and small figures held against water and open ground. View series
Snow, roads, and witness 08 works

Winter Passage

In winter the frame is stripped to essentials: road, tree, field, one or two figures, a direction of movement. Snow simplifies the scene but never the feeling. The sequence moves through witness, endurance, and the measured space between standing alone and being left alone.

Snow fields, bare trees, roads, and reduced winter geometry. View series
Crossings and walls of light 08 works

Thresholds

These works begin at thresholds: walls of light, wet pavement, railings, doorways, and the brief moment when a figure crosses into view without settling there. The city is treated not as spectacle or crowd, but as a structure of passage. Openings appear, but rarely invite entrance.

Crossings, railings, rain, walls of light, and brief urban appearances. View series
Wind, rain, and shared exposure 04 works

Weathered Nearness

Weather is not used here as mood or backdrop. It is the condition that reveals what remains when comfort disappears. Wind, rain, open ground, and shared shelter place solitude and companionship under the same pressure, where nearness becomes visible not as ease, but as exposure carried together or alone.

Weather strips relation to its most difficult form: exposure shared without sentiment. View series

Threshold Walker

Nothing Unnecessary Remains

The scene offers almost nothing that can be called excess: one tree, one reflection, one pale horizon, two quiet figures at the edge. Still, the image feels complete. Not because it contains much, but because nothing in it asks to be more than it is.

Where Solitude Becomes Visible

Standing alone is often mistaken for strength. This image suggests something quieter and more difficult: exposure without collapse, smallness without disappearance, presence without claim. The trees remain as measure, the snow as silence, and the distance between them as the place where solitude becomes visible.

Approach

Image and narration should deepen one another, not compete.

The work moves by recurrence rather than explanation: thresholds first, then open ground, reflection, weather, winter, and the rare moments when another presence changes the meaning of distance. Text appears only where it deepens the image.
