How to read this section

Stage Works is a storytelling route, not an image dump.

Series and Stage Works use the same careful sequencing, but they answer different questions. Series begins from an authored photographic idea. Stage Works begins from a performed work and follows how body, space, light, and silence unfold across time.

Route 01

Stage work

A play or performance treated as a complete photographic story, with its own context, cover, statement, and ending.

Route 02

Sequence

The order is intentional. The first image opens the threshold, the middle builds pressure, and the final image decides what remains.

Route 03

Image as fragment

Each photograph can stand alone, but its full meaning sharpens when it is read as one piece of the larger staged story.

Inquiry

Stage photography, exhibitions, and collaborations.
